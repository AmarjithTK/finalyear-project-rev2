# Predicted Load-Flow Results: Detailed Explanations

This note explains the columns in predicted_loadflow_results.csv and how each value is computed in the OpenDSS pipeline.

## 1) What is load flow (power flow)?
Load flow solves the steady-state operating point of the distribution feeder. Given:
- The circuit topology and impedances
- The load and DER injections for a specific hour
OpenDSS computes:
- Bus voltages (magnitude and angle)
- Line currents and power flows
- System losses

Mathematically, power flow solves nonlinear equations relating power injections and bus voltages.

## 2) Data source and pipeline
The results are generated by run_opendss_predicted.py using:
- A predicted input time series (predicted_results.csv)
- The feeder model (KeralaIEEE13.dss)
For each hour, the script:
1. Updates loads (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Critical)
2. Updates DERs (Solar PV, Wind generator)
3. Runs OpenDSS solve
4. Extracts voltages, line loading, losses, and grid import
5. Writes a row to predicted_loadflow_results.csv

## 3) Column-by-column explanations

### Timestamp
The hour for which the power flow was solved.

### Total_Load_Predicted_MW
Total real power demand (MW) across all four load sectors:
- Residential
- Commercial
- Industrial
- Critical

### Solar_Predicted_MW
Real power injected by the solar PV system (MW). Negative predictions are clipped to 0.

### Wind_Predicted_MW
Real power injected by the wind generator (MW). Negative predictions are clipped to 0.

### Total_DER_Predicted_MW
Total distributed generation (MW):
Solar_Predicted_MW + Wind_Predicted_MW

### Grid_Import_MW
Net real power imported from the grid (MW).
OpenDSS returns TotalPower with a sign convention where import is negative; the script multiplies by -1.

### System_Loss_MW
Total real power losses (MW) for the network, computed from OpenDSS losses in watts.

### V_Grid_650_pu
Per-unit voltage at the grid source bus (bus 650).

### V_Split_632_pu
Per-unit voltage at bus 632 (feeder split).

### V_Res_634_pu
Per-unit voltage at the residential bus (bus 634).

### V_Ind_671_pu
Per-unit voltage at the industrial bus (bus 671).

### V_Com_684_pu
Per-unit voltage at the commercial bus (bus 684).

### V_Crit_692_pu
Per-unit voltage at the critical load bus (bus 692).

### V_Solar_675_pu
Per-unit voltage at the solar PV bus (bus 675).

### V_Wind_680_pu
Per-unit voltage at the wind generator bus (bus 680).

### V_Min_pu
Minimum per-unit voltage across all buses for that hour.

### V_Max_pu
Maximum per-unit voltage across all buses for that hour.

### L_Main_650_632_pct
Line loading (%) on the main feeder segment from bus 650 to 632.

### L_Ind_632_671_pct
Line loading (%) on the industrial branch from bus 632 to 671.

### L_Solar_671_675_pct
Line loading (%) on the solar PV branch from bus 671 to 675.
This is the "L_Solar" you asked about.

### L_Wind_675_680_pct
Line loading (%) on the wind branch from bus 675 to 680.
This is the "L_Wind" you asked about.

### Max_Line_Loading_pct
The maximum of the four monitored line loadings:
L_Main_650_632_pct, L_Ind_632_671_pct, L_Solar_671_675_pct, L_Wind_675_680_pct.
This is the "max loading" you asked about.

## 4) How loading is computed
Each line loading percentage is computed as:

Loading (%) = 100 * I_max / I_rated

- I_max is the maximum phase current magnitude reported by OpenDSS
- I_rated is the line NormalAmps (thermal rating)

## 5) Voltage interpretation (per-unit)
Per-unit voltage is normalized by base voltage (1.0 pu is nominal).
Typical planning limits:
- Undervoltage: V_Min_pu < 0.95
- Overvoltage: V_Max_pu > 1.05

## 6) Common operating insights
- High Total_Load_Predicted_MW often increases Grid_Import_MW and line loading.
- High Solar/Wind reduces Grid_Import_MW and may raise voltages near their buses.
- Max_Line_Loading_pct approaching 100% indicates potential thermal overload.
- V_Min_pu below 0.95 indicates possible voltage violation.

## 7) Mapping to OpenDSS elements
Buses and elements are defined in KeralaIEEE13.dss:
- Main feeder: 650 -> 632 -> 671 -> 675 -> 680
- Industrial branch uses bus 671
- Solar PV at bus 675
- Wind generator at bus 680
- Residential bus 634, Commercial bus 684, Critical bus 692

## 8) Notes on data quality
If you see zeros in voltages or line loading fields, it usually means:
- The bus name was not found during extraction
- The line name did not exist or had no NormalAmps rating

In that case, check the DSS model consistency with the expected names in the script.
